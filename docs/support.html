Common questions

Where does my data go?
Everything you enter (names, birth details) stays on your device. Readings are generated by sending that information to Anthropic's Claude API and are not stored on any server we control. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I delete a saved profile?
Yes — swipe to delete on the People list, or remove the app entirely to clear all local data.

Why did my reading change language?
Readings are cached per language. Switching the app's language (globe icon on the home screen) regenerates the reading in the new language the next time you open it.
