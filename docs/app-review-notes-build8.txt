This build (1.0, build 8) adds a private reflection journal: users can write a personal note next to their daily tarot card and revisit it later, building a timeline of their own reflections over time rather than a read-once horoscope. This is in addition to the partner/synastry charts, multi-person profile management, and custom Destiny Matrix visualization described in our reply regarding Guideline 4.3(b). We believe these features, taken together, distinguish SoulSign from a standard single-user horoscope app. Happy to answer any questions.
