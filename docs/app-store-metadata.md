## Age Rating

Apple determines this from a questionnaire in App Store Connect (Age Rating section), not from text you write. For SoulSign, every question should be answered "None" — no violence, no mature/suggestive content, no gambling, no horror themes. That questionnaire should land the app at **4+**.
