SoulSign Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

SoulSign is a personal astrology and tarot app. This page explains what information the app handles and how it's used.

What information SoulSign collects

When you add a person to SoulSign, you provide:

  • Full name
  • Birth date and birth time
  • Birth place (city/country, and its map coordinates)

This information is entered directly by you and is only used to generate astrology and tarot readings for that person.

How this information is used

  • Generating readings. Birth details are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate natal chart readings, tarot readings, and daily affirmations. Anthropic processes this data to return the generated text; SoulSign does not run its own servers and does not store your data on any server we control. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy for how they handle API data.
  • Place search. When you type a birth place, SoulSign uses Apple's on-device MapKit search to suggest matching cities. This is handled by Apple under its own privacy terms and does not involve any third-party service from SoulSign.
  • Notifications. SoulSign can schedule a local daily reminder notification on your device. This is scheduled entirely on-device; no push notification server is used.

Where your data is stored

All profiles, birth details, and generated readings are stored locally on your device only. SoulSign has no account system, no login, and no cloud sync — your data does not leave your device except when it is sent to Anthropic's API to generate a reading, as described above.

What SoulSign does not do

  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties for marketing
  • No user accounts or passwords

Your choices

You can delete any saved profile at any time from within the app, which removes that person's data from your device. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

Children's privacy

SoulSign is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
